About Me

I’m a computer science student at Drexel University, concentrating in Computer Security and Privacy and AI & Machine Learning, with a minor in Data Science. My experience spans security operations, IT infrastructure, software development, data, and machine learning research. I’ve worked on a security team, supported self-hosted systems for a campus organization, contributed to transformer research, and built and deployed my own apps and AI-powered tools. I also work as a course assistant, helping students learn programming, Linux, and cybersecurity fundamentals. I’m interested in cybersecurity, software development, data science, systems and infrastructure, and applied AI, especially where they overlap. I like working on problems that require understanding the bigger picture, from how individual components work to how they come together as a whole. I’m drawn to work that’s practical and has a real impact, and I enjoy learning new tools and approaches along the way. I care about building technology that’s secure, reliable, and useful to the people who depend on it.
